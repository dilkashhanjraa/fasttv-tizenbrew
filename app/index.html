REMOTE GUIDE

Simple controls.

Use the remote's arrow keys to move, then press OK to open a TizenBrew module. Choose the star on any card to add or remove a favourite. Use Return/Back to close this guide.

On a keyboard, press F while a module is selected to toggle its favourite. The yellow remote key works where available.
